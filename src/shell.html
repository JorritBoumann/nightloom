Nightloom

Bramble

Move WASD · Grab or place Space · Spin, wash or throw Left Shift

Big and strong. The only one who can spin the loom and throw across the sky. Too clumsy to take stars.

Flit

Move ↑←↓→ · Grab or place Enter · Wash or dash Right Shift

Small and quick. Flies over open sky and takes stars from the shelf. Catches anything Bramble throws past.

Gamepads work too: A grabs, X works or throws, Start pauses. Esc or P pauses.
