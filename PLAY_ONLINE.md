# Play Nightloom online

Two people in two places play one shift together. You can also play with the bot as your partner.

## The link

Open **https://nightloom-production.up.railway.app/** in a desktop browser.

The game is also on the Stateloop play page as a test game (draft), for members of the Stateloop team.

## Before you start

- Use a keyboard or a gamepad. The game has no touch controls.
- Turn the sound on. The game plays a sound 4 seconds before a ready kettle turns into a nightmare.
- **Do not talk or chat with your partner while you play.** No voice call, no text chat, no talk in the same room. The game records each session to teach robots, and a talk changes the play.

## Your player code

The first time you open the online menu, the game makes a player code for you, for example `K7QX-M2PA`. The code keeps your rounds together. It is not your name.

1. Write down your player code. The online menu shows it under your name.
2. If you play on another computer or browser, select **I have a code**.
3. Type your player code, then select **Use this code**.

## Play with a partner

1. On the title screen, select **Play online**.
2. Type your name.
3. Select **Create a room**. The game shows a room code of 4 letters.
4. Give the room code to your partner.
5. Your partner selects **Play online**, types the code in the box, and selects **Join a room**.

If you do not have a partner yet, select **Quick match**. The game puts you in a room with the next player who also selects **Quick match**.

## Play with the bot

1. On the title screen, select **Play online**.
2. Type your name.
3. Select **Play with the bot**.

Note: the bot sometimes waits for ever. It holds wool and waits while the loom holds other wool. If the bot stops, take the wool off the loom.

## In the room

1. Answer the question about how you can talk to your partner. You must answer it before your first round.
2. Look at your role: **Bramble** or **Flit**. To change roles, select **Swap roles**.
3. Look at the shift. The game selects the next shift for you.
4. Select **Ready**. When both players are ready, the round starts after a count of 3.

The game selects the shifts in this order:

- Your first round is **Dusk**. It is a warm-up with hints.
- Then **Midnight**, then **Daybreak**, then Midnight again.

You can select a different shift if you want. The two players change roles after each two rounds.

## The roles

| | Bramble | Flit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speed | Slow | Fast, with a dash |
| Stars | Cannot take them | Takes them from the shelf |
| Loom | Spins wool into thread | Cannot spin |
| Open sky | Cannot cross it, but throws items over it | Flies over it and catches items in flight |
| Wind | Hardly moves | Gets blown across the room |

## Keys

Both key sets work, for both roles.

| Action | Keys | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | `W` `A` `S` `D` or the arrow keys | Left stick or D-pad |
| Grab or place | `Space` or `Enter` | A |
| Spin, wash, throw, dash | `Shift` | X or B |

The online game has no pause. Your partner keeps playing if you leave the tab.

## If something goes wrong

- If the connection stops, the game goes back to the online menu. Create a new room, or join your partner's room again.
- If your partner leaves during a round, the round ends early. The room stays open for a new partner.
- If a room code does not work, make sure that the room is still open and has a free place.

## What the game records

The game records each round on the Stateloop server: every key press, every position and every event in the game. It also records your player code, your browser type, your screen size and your network delay. It keeps your name in a separate file, apart from the play data. When you join a room, you agree to this recording.
